Changelog

Every release.

What changed in each version, newest first. Snapframe updates itself from inside the app.

1.7.0Nov 10, 2026
  • NewChoose your preview card style in Settings under Appearance: Classic, or Hover Controls with a clean preview and controls that appear when you point at it.
  • NewSave from the Hover Controls card to keep a copy anywhere.
  • NewResize floating pins by dragging the corner. They keep their shape and stay on screen.
  • NewAn opacity slider on floating pins, so you can see through one to compare captures.
1.6.1Nov 10, 2026
  • NewSix themes in Settings under Appearance: System, Graphite, Daylight, Safelight, Deep Sea and Aurora.
  • NewSix accent colors that apply across the whole app.
  • NewA background picker in Annotate and Studio with None, Solid and Gradient, including 48 solid colors and 30 gradients.
  • NewMake your own solid color or gradient with any angle, and save it to Your colors.
  • ImprovedText and accent colors stay readable on every theme.
  • FixedA capture whose saved file was deleted in Finder now leaves the library too. Files on an unplugged drive are kept.
1.5.0Nov 10, 2026
  • NewStudio mode in the Record panel. Your cursor, clicks, shortcuts and camera are kept apart from the screen, so they can all be changed after recording.
  • NewTimeline editor with split, trim and delete, plus full undo and redo.
  • NewAutomatic zoom into the moments you clicked. Every zoom can be moved, resized or removed.
  • NewRestyled cursor with pointer, dot and ring styles, size, smoothing and hide when idle.
  • NewStyled backgrounds with colors, gradients, padding, rounded corners and shadow.
  • NewMove, resize or mirror your camera after recording, and set microphone and system volume separately.
  • NewMP4 export at the original size, 1440p, 1080p or 720p, rendered entirely on your Mac.
  • ImprovedStudio recordings are recovered after a crash or forced quit, and are never auto-deleted.
  • ImprovedThe preview card and Home open Studio projects directly in the editor.
1.4.0Nov 10, 2026
  • NewRecord your screen or any area to MP4 or GIF
  • NewSeparate microphone and system audio tracks
  • NewWebcam bubble, click highlights and shortcut display while recording
  • NewPause, resume, restart and discard from a control bar, with the time in the menu bar
  • NewAutomatic recovery of recordings after a crash or forced quit
  • NewTrim recordings from Home or the preview card
  • NewRecording settings for frame rate and maximum resolution
  • ImprovedHome and the capture toolbar now include Record
1.3.0Nov 10, 2026
  • NewScrolling capture that stitches long pages and skips sidebars and fixed bars
  • NewOn-device text recognition that copies text straight to the clipboard
  • NewQR code reading, with web links you can choose to open
  • NewHide desktop icons on demand, or only while capturing
  • Newsnapframe:// links for scripts and launchers, with your approval
  • NewShortcuts for scrolling capture, text capture and desktop icons
  • ImprovedCapture toolbar now includes Scrolling and Text modes
1.2.0Nov 10, 2026
  • NewAnnotate editor with crop, arrows, lines, shapes, drawing, marker, text, counters, spotlight, redaction and images
  • NewBackgrounds with colors, gradients, padding, corner radius and shadow
  • NewUndo and redo, and captures stay editable when you reopen them
  • NewSnapframe project files that open with a double-click
  • NewPassword-protected PDF export and the macOS share sheet
  • NewOpen any image in the editor from Home
  • NewChoose Edit after a capture to go straight to the editor
  • ImprovedRedactions are permanently burned into saved edits and projects
1.1.0Nov 10, 2026
  • NewHome window with capture tiles, recent captures, search and a details panel
  • NewPrivate capture library with tags, pinning and auto-delete
  • NewMenu bar popover and a preview card after each capture
  • NewFloat any capture above other windows
  • ImprovedCapture overlay with aspect ratios, a color readout and after-capture choices
  • ImprovedRedesigned Settings with a visual file name builder
  • ImprovedFour-step onboarding and a clearer update window
1.0.2Nov 9, 2026
  • Fixedopening Snapframe while it is already running now shows Settings instead of appearing to do nothing
  • Added a message at launch showing that Snapframe is in the menu bar
  • Snapframe now checks for updates every time it starts
1.0.1Nov 9, 2026
  • Added the Snapframe app icon
  • Added screenshots to the README
1.0.0Nov 9, 2026
  • Capture an area, window or full screen, or repeat the previous area
  • Self-timer, freeze screen, crosshair and loupe
  • Save as PNG, JPEG or HEIC, or copy to the clipboard
  • Custom file names and global shortcuts
  • In-app updates from GitHub releases
